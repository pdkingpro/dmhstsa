Frequently Asked Questions

A quick guide to joining, competing, and getting involved with our chapter.

How do I join?
Attend one of our meetings, talk with the advisor, and complete the chapter registration and membership steps. New members are always welcome.
What is the membership cost?
Membership fees are shared with students at the start of the school year. Please ask the advisor for the current amount and payment deadline.
When do meetings happen?
Meeting dates are posted in announcements and shared during school. Check the chapter calendar and follow the advisor's reminders.
Who is eligible to compete?
Any active chapter member who has completed registration and is approved by the advisor may be eligible to compete, depending on the event rules and school policies.
Is there a dress code?
Members should wear school-appropriate attire for chapter meetings. For competitions, the advisor will provide any required dress or presentation expectations.
What about travel information?
Travel plans, transportation details, and hotel information for conferences are shared by the advisor as events approach. Students should check announcements regularly.
